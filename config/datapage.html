Access WHOI Spray Glider Data

Most of our data are publicly available. While this site provides utilties for viewing and plotting the data, downloads of data are enabled by partnerships with other groups.

Near-Real-Time Data:

Near-real-time data from our gliders are accessible via the US IOOS Glider Data Assembly Center.

Post-Processed Data:

To access post-processed data in NetCDF format with permanent citation information, please visit spraydata.ucsd.edu. Post-processed data should be used for nearly all analyses.

Project-specific data sets include:

  • Todd, R.E., W.B. Owens (2016), Gliders in the Gulf Stream [Data set]. Scripps Institution of Oceanography, Instrument Development Group, doi: 10.21238/S8SPRAY2675.
  • Todd, R.E. (2020), Spray glider observations in support of NASCar [Data set]. Scripps Institution of Oceanography, Instrument Development Group, doi: 10.21238/S8SPRAY0053.
  • Todd, R.E. (2020), Spray glider observations in support of PEACH [Data set]. Scripps Institution of Oceanography, Instrument Development Group, doi: 10.21238/S8SPRAY0880.
  • Todd, R.E. (2024). Spray glider observations in support of MAB-SWOT [Data set]. Scripps Institution of Oceanography, Instrument Development Group. doi: 10.21238/S8PP8C.

Data Products:

We have also published the following data products:

  • Todd, R.E., A.S. Ren (2023), Gulf Stream mean and annual cycle from Spray underwater glider measurements [Data set]. Scripps Institution of Oceanography, Instrument Development Group. doi: 10.21238/S8TC9W.
